## Re-scoring of the v0.2 outputs

After the rerun, a second review found verification-side defects; these were fixed and the stored v0.2 outputs re-scored without any model calls (`audit/rescore.py`).

- **One context per comparison.** The prompt rendered the paper text from `choose_proof()`, but paper quotes were verified against a different concatenation of texts. Both now use one immutable context object. Re-scored against it, 4 of 3,050 difference quotes and 3 of 3,206 extracted-field quotes changed status.
- **Quote normalization keeps brace grouping** (`x^{n+1}` no longer matches `x^n+1`), and each quote has one of three statuses: exact, normalized, unresolved-OCR.
- **Page-level status is the headline.** Of 3,050 paper quotes in reported differences, 2,430 are found on the page their citation points to (2,330 exact, 100 normalized, 620 unresolved-OCR), and 3,040 in the text the model was shown (2,905 exact, 135 normalized, 10 unresolved-OCR); 613 pass in the context but fail on the cited page.
- **Grouping includes the Lean file**, not just line numbers. This split 2 groups that mixed a declaration's lines with same-numbered lines of a definition in another file; 4 of the resulting groups have no adjudication of their own and are reported as "not adjudicated".
- **Run id on every row** (in the `model` column), and report queries read one run only. The response cache key now includes temperature and token budget; responses logged before this change are reused only with `AUDIT_LLM_LEGACY_CACHE=1`.

## Agreement

v0.1 counted a difference as "reported by all 3 samples" when 3 samples cited overlapping Lean lines. v0.2 keeps that count as `overlapping_citations` and adds an adjudication pass: for every group with 2 or more samples, 3 further samples answer only whether the descriptions assert the same difference. `agreement` is reported only where all 3 said yes.
